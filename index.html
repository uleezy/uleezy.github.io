short bio

Ula is a San Diego rapper and producer building a sound somewhere between West Coast rap, G-Funk, glitch-hop, funk, underground hip-hop, and a busted JRPG save file with too much soul in it.

Raised in Southern California, Ula brings together family-rooted music history, off-grid producer instincts, and a personality that moves between half nerd and half ghetto fabulous. His music is rhythmic, personal, weird, and homegrown.
